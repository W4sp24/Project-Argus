`## Questions` — eight to fifteen questions, each as a `###` heading phrased
the way a confused reader would actually ask it, followed by its answer in two
to five sentences.

Ask what the material is *for*, not what it *contains*: "Why does normalising
the input matter?" rather than "What is normalisation?". Order them so an
early answer never depends on a later one.

Where the material answers a question only partly, say so in the answer rather
than completing it yourself.
