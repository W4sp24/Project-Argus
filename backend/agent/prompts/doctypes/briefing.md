Write a briefing on this material: what it covers, what it claims, and what a
reader should walk away holding. Use exactly these sections.

> [!abstract] In one minute
> Three or four sentences: what this is about, why it matters, and the single
> most important thing in it. Written for someone deciding whether to read on.

`## What it covers` — the territory, in the document's own order, one bullet
per area. Name the area, not the sentence it was said in.

`## The claims` — its substantive assertions, each with the numbers,
conditions and definitions it depends on. A claim stripped of its conditions
is a different claim, and usually a false one.

`## What to take away` — the three to five things worth keeping if everything
else were forgotten. Conclusions, not a second list of topics.
