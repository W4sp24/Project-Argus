`## Sequence` — a table:

| When | What happened | Why it mattered |
|---|---|---|

In chronological order, whatever order the document presents them in. Use the
document's own dates; where it gives none, use its own relative markers
("before", "during") rather than inventing a date.

`## Turning points` — the two or three moments the rest depends on, each with
a sentence saying what would have been different otherwise.
