Map how the ideas in this material relate to each other. The reader already
knows the definitions; what they are missing is the shape. Use exactly these
sections.

> [!abstract] In one minute
> What this material is about, and what its central organising idea is.

`## The ideas` — one bullet per idea, bolded, with a one-line statement of it.

`## How they connect` — one bullet per relationship, each naming two ideas and
the relationship between them: *depends on*, *is a special case of*,
*contradicts*, *generalises*, *is the converse of*. State the direction. "X
and Y are related" is not a relationship.

`## The through-line` — a short paragraph walking from the most basic idea to
the most advanced one, in the order a reader should meet them.
